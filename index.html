Overview

Zion AI Security Posture unifies findings from your CSPM, IAM, endpoint, and vulnerability tools into one explainable score — per team, per app, per environment — so leaders see risk at a glance and engineers get prioritized, owned fixes.

Key capabilities

Unified scoring: one explainable score aggregating every security signal source.
Trend & benchmark: posture over time, compared against industry peers.
Prioritized remediation: fixes routed to owners with SLA tracking.
Executive reporting: board-ready risk summaries generated automatically.
